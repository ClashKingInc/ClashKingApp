# Clan clearing background

User-approved generated artwork, 22 October 2026. This is not an official
Supercell asset. Generated with the built-in image tool, using the actual
`clashking_assets/assets/buildings/home-village/clan_castle/level_8.webp`
as a visual reference. Earlier two candidates were rejected and are not used.

Brief: preserve the reference's squat cubic Clan Castle, blue courtyard,
gold trim and chunky slate buttresses. Place it in a simple grassy Clash-style
village clearing, elevated three-quarter view, with a few pines, rocks and
short walls. Leave quiet foreground for UI. No banners, flags, characters,
builders, barbarians, text or UI. Avoid realistic or elaborate fantasy masonry.

Approved PNG: `clan-background-candidate-3.png` in the task's visualization
directory. Delivery: 1672 × 941 WebP, quality 88, cover-cropped by the native
image component. Original generation is retained outside the repository.

The tint is separate, not baked into the artwork. Recreate tint PNGs with
`node scripts/generate-header-scrims.mjs` from the Expo root.
