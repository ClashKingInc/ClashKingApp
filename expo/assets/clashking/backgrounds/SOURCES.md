# Ranked checker backgrounds

Exported from the user's `clash_checkered_background_large.psd` (4879 × 3658).
Each WebP is the PSD's composed `blue`, `purple`, `red`, or `green` group with
the other top-level color groups hidden. The texture was not reconstructed from
the separate grayscale tiled PSD. Original shading and gradient layers remain.
Delivery images are 960 × 720 WebP, quality 88, reduced from the 1600 × 1200
exports. The shared header renderer crops at a 700-point minimum canvas (30%
smaller pattern than the earlier 1000-point canvas). It uses native images and a
tiny pre-rendered light/dark fade, with no layout measurement or live SVG.
Assets warm in the background after fonts load; navigation never awaits them.

Color use: green for Town Hall and donations; red for war records; purple for
ranked players, ranked/Capital/clan-home boards; blue for Legend player pages,
home-village player trophies, and Builder boards. The source's blue group has
a blue-violet appearance; purple has the plum/magenta appearance.

Stats headers use red for war and armies, purple for ranked, green for clans,
and blue for CWL, items and players.
